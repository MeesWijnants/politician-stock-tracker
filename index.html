Politician Stock Tracker: Who in Congress Trades the Most?

Every member of the U.S. Congress must report stock trades above $1,000 within 45 days. That makes Congress one of the few groups of investors whose trades are public. This page ranks the most active traders and shows where to follow them. For every politician and every filing, use the politician stock tracker at Wolf of Washington.

What about Nancy Pelosi?

The most searched name is not the most active trader. The Nancy Pelosi stock tracker shows 211 trades, far fewer than Khanna, but often large option positions in big tech, mostly filed for her husband. Her estimated Nancy Pelosi net worth is about $142.3 million (2025), the highest on our page.

How to read a trade correctly

Three things matter. Amounts are ranges, never exact figures. The owner code shows whether the trade was made by the member, a spouse or a child. And up to 45 days can pass before a trade is public.
